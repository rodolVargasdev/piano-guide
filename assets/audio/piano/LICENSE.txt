Salamander Grand Piano (subset, converted to MP3)

Author: Alexander Holm
Original: Salamander Grand Piano V3, https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
Copy used here: the 30 MP3 files of the Tone.js audio repository, folder salamander/
  (https://github.com/Tonejs/audio, served at https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/).
  The hosted README of that folder names the V2 lineage of the set.
Selection: one velocity layer, one sample every minor third (A0, C1, Ds1, Fs1, A1, ... A7, C8),
  where Ds and Fs stand for D sharp and F sharp. The files are used unchanged, as downloaded
  on 2026-10-11. The SHA-256 of each file is in manifest.json and in docs/STACK_TECNOLOGICO.md.
