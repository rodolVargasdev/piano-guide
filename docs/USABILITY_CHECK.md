# Usability check: Learn mode

A manual protocol to find out whether a person who has never played the piano can learn a first phrase with Learn mode. Nothing here is measured by the tests: it needs a person, a device and a clock.

Revised on: 2026-10-11 (initial thresholds, proposed and not yet measured). Changing a threshold after a session is an edit to this file with the reason written next to it, so a revision never happens silently.

## Participant

- Has never played a piano or keyboard (not even a little), and does not read music.
- Is not shown the app, the song or the task beforehand.
- One person at a time, with the observer sitting beside them.

## Setup

1. Device: the one the participant will really use (laptop, tablet or phone). Record which.
2. Input: start with on-screen keys. For a laptop also say that the computer keys play, but do not explain how. Record which input they used.
3. Language: the participant chooses English or Spanish in the selector. Record which.
4. Start from the catalog, with no saved progress (Reset progress, or a clean browser profile).
5. Screen recording or a stopwatch. Start the clock when the task is read aloud.

For T2 the private song must be available on the observer's machine (the local library, see the README). It is never shown to anyone as a recording or a screenshot in a public place.

## Rules for the observer

- Read the task aloud once, exactly as written below. Do not explain, point, hint or answer questions about the screen. If asked, say "do what you think is right".
- Write down every hesitation of more than 10 seconds and every question the participant asks.
- Stop a task when it passes its time limit; record it as not passed.
- Do not count a key pressed by the observer.

## Tasks

| Task | Read aloud | Time limit | Available |
|------|------------|------------|-----------|
| T1 | "Find the note C on the piano." (lesson zero) | 2 min | now (the "Lesson zero" button on the first screen of any song) |
| T2 | "Play the first part of this song with your right hand." Once in the original key, once in C major (use the key toggle) | 10 min per key | now (private Cancer on the observer's machine) |
| T3 | "Play the first phrase of Ode to Joy." | 5 min | after Milestone B (Ode to Joy) |
| T4 | Close the page in the middle of a phrase. "Open it again and carry on where you were." | 2 min | now |
| T5 | "Do the 10-minute routine." | 10 min | after Milestone C (practice exercises) |

## What to record, per task

- Time to finish (or "not finished" at the limit).
- Number of hesitations longer than 10 seconds.
- Number of questions asked.
- Number of wrong keys (the app shows "Try again").
- Anything the participant said aloud that shows confusion, in their own words.

## Pass thresholds

A task passes when all of these hold:

| Task | Finished within | Hesitations over 10 s | Questions | Wrong keys |
|------|-----------------|-----------------------|-----------|------------|
| T1 | 2 min | at most 1 | 0 | at most 5 |
| T2 (each key) | 10 min | at most 3 | 0 | at most 15 |
| T3 | 5 min | at most 2 | 0 | at most 10 |
| T4 | 2 min | 0 | 0 | not counted |
| T5 | 10 min | at most 3 | 0 | not counted |

The check as a whole passes when T2 and T4 pass with two different participants, and T3 and T5 pass once they are available (T1 already is).

## After the session

Write the results in the tracker of the change (date, device, input, language, one row per task), and list every sentence of the screen the participant did not understand. A failed task is a defect of the screen, not of the participant.
