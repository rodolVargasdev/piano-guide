## What changes

- `tests/fixtures/chords-only-song.json` (new, data): a synthetic chords-only song, 8 bars in A major with invented chords (A E F#m D A E G A; the G is borrowed so it carries an accidental), a Beginner level (one bass note and a right-hand triad per bar, editorial fingering) and an Original level (a bass pattern, a held chord, analysis entries), a lesson per level, placeholder sources and confidence per section. No melody, no lyrics, not a real song. The fixture was generated by a script and validated with `validateSong` (no error, no warning).
- `tests/songs.test.js`: every guard that read the public song now reads the fixture, each with its counter-proof: no lyrics key and no melody role (and a copy with each is rejected), every note is a tone of the chord at its tick (and a wrong note is found), hand positions name notes the level plays (and one that does not is found), the lesson lists the chords of the track and each is recognized, the Original level is engraved with strict bars and accompaniment and bass roles only, each bar has a four-note bass pattern and a held chord with every chord tone, the bar starts on the chord's bass, analysis numerals and functions agree with the chord track (and a wrong numeral is detected). One new generic guard: every song of the public index records https sources with kind and date, and confidence per scope.
- `tests/score/engraving-polish.test.js`: the accidental-overlap guard runs on Chopin and on the fixture, both levels.

Removed on purpose (they asserted the content of that one song, not a rule of the data model): its chord progression, its sources by name, its borrowed-chord analysis text, its voice-leading claims and the counter-proof of those claims. Their rules (no invented chord, analysis agrees with the chord track) remain through the generic guards above.

## Counter-proofs

Kept one for one: lyrics and melody copy, wrong chord tone (two ways), hand position at a note the level does not play, wrong analysis numeral. The temporary rename check above is the counter-proof that the dependency is really gone.
